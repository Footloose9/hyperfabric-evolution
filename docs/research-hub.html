03 / AUTONOMY◇

Privacy and personal agency

Research information minimization, confidentiality, consent and verifiable voluntary disclosure without equating privacy with wrongdoing.

MoneroZanoMetadataSelective disclosure
Explore privacy-specific evidence limits

Monero and Zano use privacy mechanisms that hide transaction properties from ordinary public observers. A transparent account-graph approach cannot recover what was not disclosed. Network and off-chain metadata remain separate risks.
