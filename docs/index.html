OUR FIRST LIMITED LIVE CHAIN PREVIEW

XRPL: inspect public ledger data, read-only.

Unlike the fictional graph above, this standalone viewer makes manual, read-only requests to a public XRP Ledger provider. The result is a provider report, not independently verified consensus.

FUNCTIONAL PUBLIC PREVIEW / NONCOMMERCIAL / NO WALLET

Validated ledger and public native-XRP balances

Query a provider-reported validated ledger hash and close time, or enter a public classic address to inspect its native XRP balance pinned to that ledger. Unavailable, stale, and inconsistent results are refused rather than replaced with synthetic values.

Open the manual XRPL viewer ↗

No login, wallet connection, transaction submission, automatic polling, or signing. The provider may be unavailable or rate-limit requests.

What the viewer does not establish

  • Provider-reported validated is not an independent proof of network consensus or uninterrupted freshness.
  • The public RPC operator can observe your IP address and the public account you query.
  • Only native XRP balance is displayed. Issued assets require currency + issuer; MPTs use a distinct identity.
  • No seed, recovery phrase, or private key should ever be entered. This is not a wallet or financial execution service.

PUBLIC RESEARCH PILOT

Evidence before allegations.

Our first community research case is a historical BITx-related XRP Ledger event. We invite independent replication of ledger facts, legitimate-arbitrage counterexamples, and corrections to the method—not speculative identification of people. Consequential conclusions require independent human review.
