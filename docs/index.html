THE RESEARCH MISSION

Make claims inspectable.

Blockchain transparency should include the method, its assumptions, and its limits—not just a chart or an automated label.

⌗

01 / VERIFY

Precise identities

Preserve network, issuer, issuance and contract identity. An asset ticker alone is not a source of truth.

02 / TRACE

Typed evidence graphs

Follow explicitly sourced connections without confusing transfers, fee sponsorship or shared venues with common control.

Adversarial research

Test ordinary alternatives, missing data and provider disagreement before advancing an anomaly hypothesis.

PUBLIC RESEARCH PILOT

Evidence before allegations.

Our first community research case is a historical BITx-related XRP Ledger event. We invite independent replication of ledger facts, legitimate-arbitrage counterexamples, and corrections to the method—not speculative identification of people. Consequential conclusions require independent human review.
