# Community conduct

Hyperfabric Evolution welcomes questions, review, principled disagreement, and technical criticism. Contributions should be civil, evidence-based, and focused on publicly verifiable work.

- Critique claims, methods, and code, not a person's identity or characteristics.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, dox, or publish identifying personal information.
- Do not share secrets, private project information, other people's wallet credentials, confidential logs, or private security vulnerabilities.
- Do not present inference or blockchain address clustering as proof of a person's identity or misconduct.
- Do not use issues or discussions to solicit investment, coordinate trading, promote tokens, or post spam.
- Credit public sources and respect third-party intellectual property.

Moderators may edit, lock, or remove unsuitable material and restrict repeat violations. Report sensitive security matters privately per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in public issues. Project participation does not grant access to private systems.
